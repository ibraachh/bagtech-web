Questions

Frequently asked questions

What services does BagTech provide?

BagTech is an IT company based in Baku, Azerbaijan. We provide custom software development, mobile apps, AI solutions, cybersecurity, CRM and ERP systems, system integration, web development, IT infrastructure and IT outsourcing.

What should you look for when choosing an IT company?

Real projects (a portfolio), stages and budget documented up front, support after handover, and a team that understands your industry.

How does a project start?

With a free initial consultation. Then come the audit and requirements (1–2 weeks) and the architecture and proposal (1 week). Stages, timeline and budget are documented before anything is agreed.

How is the price of a project calculated?

It depends on the scope, integrations and platforms. There is no fixed price list: each project gets its own proposal based on the requirements, and the initial estimate is free.

Do you only work with companies in Baku?

No. Azerbaijan is our main market, and we also work remotely with clients abroad.
