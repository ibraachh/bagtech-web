Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do you need to start developing a mobile app?

A description of the main scenario (what the user will do in the app), the target platforms and information about your existing systems are enough. The rest — requirements gathering, design and architecture — we work out together at the audit stage.

Should I choose a native or a cross-platform app?

It depends on the project's requirements, and we make the choice together with you. Cross-platform (React Native, Flutter) covers both platforms with a single codebase and is usually quicker to deliver. Native (Swift) suits apps that need to make the most of the device's capabilities and deliver high performance.

How much does it cost to develop a mobile app?

The cost depends on the number of screens and features, the platforms, the integrations and the design. The initial estimate is free; we usually recommend starting with an MVP version.

Do you publish the app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We handle publishing the app in the stores and the updates that follow.
