Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where does a cybersecurity audit start?

It starts with a review of your existing systems, access rights and backups. The result is a clear list of weak spots and priorities.

Does a small company need cybersecurity?

Yes. Most attacks begin with a weak password, software that hasn't been updated or a phishing email; these risks don't depend on the size of the company.

What is a pentest (vulnerability test)?

It is a check of your systems from an attacker's point of view. The goal is to find and close the weak spots before a real attack.

What cybersecurity measures should a company take first?

Start with two-factor authentication, working backups and timely updates: together they significantly reduce the most common risks.
