Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is AI used in business?

The areas that deliver the most are AI assistants that answer customer questions, automatic processing of documents and data, data-driven forecasting and analytics, and access and attendance control with face recognition. Adoption starts with choosing a specific problem and running a small pilot project.

How does a face recognition system work?

The camera detects a face and converts it into a digital signature, which is then compared with the signatures in the database. Adding a new person to the system takes about 15 seconds.

Where is face recognition data stored?

On the company's own server: the system runs there and the data is not sent to an external cloud. Because it runs fast even on an ordinary laptop, no special server is required.

Can an AI assistant work with our existing systems?

Yes. The assistant integrates with your website, CRM and other systems via API.
