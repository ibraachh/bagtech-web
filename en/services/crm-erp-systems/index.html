Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an ERP system?

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is software that brings a company's internal operations — HR, finance, inventory, purchasing and sales — together in one system. The aim is for every department to work with the same data.

What is a CRM system and how is it different from ERP?

A CRM manages customer relationships and sales: the customer base, deal stages and tasks. An ERP covers the company's internal resources. In growing companies the two work together through integration.

Should we use a ready-made ERP platform or build a system from scratch?

If your processes are close to standard, customising a ready-made platform such as ERPNext is faster and more cost-effective. For a non-standard business model, a system built from scratch is a better fit.

What are the stages of CRM and ERP implementation?

Implementation moves through an audit, a pilot in one department, data migration, staff training and a phased rollout to the other departments.

Can a CRM or ERP be integrated with our existing software?

Yes: your website, payment systems, accounting and other services are connected via API.
